AICAIML — AI Career Readiness & Certification Track
Full Syllabus & Interview Guide (Member Resource)
=================================================

COURSE OVERVIEW
---------------
Turns technical skill into a hireable portfolio, and guides you
through the AICAIML professional certification pathway.

MODULE BREAKDOWN
-----------------
1. Building a Portfolio That Gets Noticed
   - What reviewers screen for first
   - Common gaps that get applications filtered out
   - Presenting honest, well-scoped results over inflated claims

2. Technical Interview Preparation
   - Structuring answers under time pressure
   - System-design reasoning for ML applications
   - Practice question bank and self-review checklist

3. Certification Exam Format
   - Coverage areas and scoring breakdown
   - Time allocation and pacing strategy
   - Retake policy

4. Verified Credentialing
   - How certificates are issued after passing
   - Unique verification code format
   - How employers can confirm a certificate is genuine

INTERVIEW ANSWER STRUCTURE (QUICK REFERENCE)
---------------------------------------------
1. Clarify requirements and constraints before proposing a solution
2. State assumptions explicitly
3. Walk through trade-offs, not just one "correct" answer
4. Close with how you would validate the solution works

ASSESSMENT
----------
Complete the in-course quiz (3 questions, 70% pass mark) to mark this
course complete and become eligible for the AICAIML certificate for
this track.
